## Judge Q&A

- **How accurate is the photo check?** We don't claim an accuracy number; we have no hand labels. Every check shows the photo it used, and when the photo is unclear Hermit says "couldn't tell" and turns it into a question for the landlord.
- **Isn't this a listing filter?** A filter matches fields. Hermit builds a room graph from each scan, lays her layout on the real floor plan, checks the aerial and the photos, rules spaces out on evidence and writes what's left to do.
- **What about garage parking?** It can't be counted from the air, and Hermit says so. The count is labeled "around the building" unless it's clipped to the lot.
- **Is it available?** A listing on record isn't live availability. Hermit shows the listing it found and drafts the question; the broker confirms.
- **Why CoStar?** Three layers answer three questions: CoStar says what the building is and was, Matterport what's built inside, Vexcel what the site supports. Most of that isn't on the open web, so a public-data agent can't do this.
- **How is it agentic?** It plans the search, picks which checks to run, looks closer when it's unsure, rules spaces out on evidence and changes its top pick, with the reason in "How Hermit decided".
- **Why not just ChatGPT?** ChatGPT can't see inside these buildings or count their parking. Hermit's model only plans; the numbers come from deterministic tools over CoStar records, Matterport scans and Vexcel imagery, and each one links to its source.
- **Where do costs come from?** A sourced screening table, shown as ranges. The headline is weeks to open.

## Don't say

- "compliant", "fraud", "guaranteed", "verified" for anything a scan can't see
- "cut the middleman", "replace brokers", "replace agents"
- "multi-agent", "a crew of agents", "our agents": it's one agent that runs specialist checks
- "Only 25% of CoStar's data is used" (the stat is about content not being on the open web)
- "Rufus makes people buy 60% more" (it's a correlation)
- "Spark" for Walmart's assistant (it's Sparky)
- that Hermit cuts CoStar's research cost
- that a space is available, or that wiring, gas, drains or grease interceptors are fine (scans can't see behind walls)
- "own clinic" on screen (the copy check flags it); say "her own practice" or "open her own"
- any number that isn't on screen or in the source table above, including invented funnel counts or savings
- a photo-check accuracy number
